This is Teaching Genetics, a Minecraft mod designed to simplify the basics of genetics understanding through our favorite blocky game. It will include an interactive book, with drag and drop Punnett Squares and hover-over-that-word definitions. This is geared towards younger children, but it can be utilized by anyone who has never been exposed to genetics (or was and does not remember)!

The IDE used is IntelliJ Community Edition, and Parchement mappings were utilized as well.
This mod is for 1.20.1
